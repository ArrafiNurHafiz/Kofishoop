## Rules — apply to ALL prose output
- No "delve", "leverage", "utilize", "comprehensive", "robust", "seamless"
- No em-dash overuse. No bullet walls. No "certainly!" or "great question!"
- No hedging phrases: "it's worth noting", "it's important to remember"
- Write like a senior engineer explaining to a peer, not an AI performing helpfulness
- Short sentences. Direct verbs. Specific nouns.
- If a word can be cut without loss of meaning: cut it.
